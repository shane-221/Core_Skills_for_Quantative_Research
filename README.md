# Core Skills for Quantitative Research

This repository tracks my ongoing development in the core skills needed for quantitative and empirical research, with a focus on applications in economics, markets, and data‑driven analysis.

It brings together structured notes, worked examples, and small projects across:
- mathematics
- economics
- research methods

The goal is to build a clear, reproducible foundation for future work in macro/quant research, research engineering, and related roles.

---

## 📚 What this repo contains

### 1. Mathematics
Conceptual and applied notes on:
- calculus
- linear algebra
- probability
- statistics


### 2. Economics
Structured notes on:
- macroeconomics(Demand Supply,Trade, Finance, Ways to see the econnomy, Government Intervnetion)

### 3. Research Methods
Concise notes on:
- what research is and how it is framed
- research paradigms (ontology, epistemology, theory)
- research design and ethics
- data collection (qualitative and quantitative)
- data analysis
- reporting and interpretation
